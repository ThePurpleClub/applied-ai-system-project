# Mood notes

## Happy
profile: mood=happy; energy=0.75; valence=0.8
Happy music is bright and upbeat.

## Sad
profile: mood=sad; energy=0.4; valence=0.3
Sad music is low valence and usually not loud.

## Chill
profile: mood=chill; energy=0.35; valence=0.55
Chill music is soft and low energy.

## Intense
profile: mood=intense; energy=0.9; valence=0.45
Intense music is loud, driving, and high energy.

## Moody
profile: mood=moody; energy=0.6; valence=0.45
Moody music sits in the middle: not a party and not a lullaby.
