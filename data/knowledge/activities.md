# Listening situations

Short guides for requests that name an activity instead of a genre.

## Late-night drive
profile: mood=moody; energy=0.65; valence=0.45; mood_tags=late-night
A late-night drive wants moody, mid-energy music with a nocturnal feel. Prefer songs tagged late-night. Keep the volume steady rather than cheerful or sleepy.

## Workout
profile: mood=intense; energy=0.9; valence=0.7; mood_tags=pumped
A workout, gym session, or run wants intense, high-energy, pumped tracks. Loud and driving is better than calm.

## Study
profile: mood=chill; energy=0.35; valence=0.55; language=none; mood_tags=calm
Studying, focus, or homework wants calm, low-energy, instrumental music with no lyrics. Lofi and ambient fits. Language none means wordless.

## Rainy day
profile: mood=chill; energy=0.4; valence=0.5; mood_tags=rainy
A rainy day wants mellow, soft, indoor music. Not a party and not a workout.
