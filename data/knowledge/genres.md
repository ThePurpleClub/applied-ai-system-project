# Genre notes

## Pop
profile: genre=pop; mood=happy; energy=0.7
Pop is bright, hook-led, and usually high or medium energy.

## Blues
profile: genre=blues; mood=sad; energy=0.45; valence=0.35
Blues leans sad or moody, with lower valence than pop.

## Metal
profile: genre=metal; mood=intense; energy=0.9
Metal and heavy metal are intense and high energy.

## Lofi
profile: genre=lofi; mood=chill; energy=0.35; language=none
Lofi is calm, often instrumental, and good for studying.
